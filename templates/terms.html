Terms of Use

Plain English. Last updated October 2026.

Drawings are estimates

The CAD drawings and measurements come from a single photograph. Camera angle, lens distortion, and perspective all introduce error. These are estimates — always verify with a physical measurement before cutting, printing, or ordering anything sized to your foot.

Not medical advice

This scanner measures foot shape and size only. It is not a medical device, it is not certified for any medical use, and nothing it produces is medical advice. Do not use it for medical decisions — talk to a qualified professional.

Service as-is

The service is provided as-is, without warranties of any kind. We aim for accurate drawings, but we can't guarantee any particular result.

Acceptable use

Use the scanner for measuring your own feet (or someone else's, with their permission). Don't try to abuse, overload, or reverse-engineer the service.
