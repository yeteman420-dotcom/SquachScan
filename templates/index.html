How it works

  • Place a reference (quarter or card) beside your foot
  • Take a top-down photo with your phone
  • Get foot length, ball/heel/arch widths in mm + inches

Set up your scan

Which foot?
Reference object
A reference object is required for real measurements. Without one, you'll get the foot shape only — no millimeters, no inches.

Photo tips

  • Plain, contrasting floor or a light sheet under your foot
  • Hold the phone directly above your foot, pointing straight down
  • Good, even light — avoid shadows across the foot
  • Place the quarter/card beside your foot, not under it
  • Bare foot, toes visible, whole foot in frame
