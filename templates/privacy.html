Privacy Policy

Plain English. Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

1. Your foot photo. When you run a scan, your photo is held in the server's memory only while your CAD drawing is being generated. It is permanently deleted immediately after. It is never stored on disk, never backed up, and never written to any log.

2. A consent record. When you tap "I consent," we keep a timestamp and a random ID proving you agreed. That's it — no photo, no name, no email, no account.

What we never do

  • No accounts and no sign-ups — there's nothing to sign into.
  • No analytics, no trackers, no cookies.
  • We never sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • The measurements and drawing belong to you — download them; we don't keep copies.

Location data

Before your photo ever leaves your phone, this app strips all EXIF metadata (including GPS location) from the image. The photo we receive has no location data in it.

Deletion

Photos are deleted automatically, every time, seconds after your drawing is made — you don't have to ask. To delete your consent record (timestamp + random ID), email squachacers@gmail.com and we'll remove it.

Contact

Questions about privacy: squachacers@gmail.com
